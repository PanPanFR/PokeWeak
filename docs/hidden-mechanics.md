# Common Hidden Battle Mechanics

## Type Immunities

### Fire-type
- Cannot be Burned.

### Electric-type
- Cannot be Paralyzed.

### Poison & Steel-type
- Cannot be Poisoned.

### Grass-type
- Immune to Powder moves.
- Immune to Leech Seed.

### Ghost-type
- Cannot be trapped.

### Dark-type
- Immune to opposing Prankster-boosted status moves.

### Rock-type
- Gains 1.5× Special Defense during Sandstorm.

### Ice-type
- Gains 1.5× Defense during Snow.

---

## Move Interactions

### Rage Powder
Does not affect:
- Grass-types
- Pokémon with Overcoat
- Pokémon holding Safety Goggles

### Fake Out
- Only works on the first turn the user is on the field.

### Protect
- Consecutive uses have a lower success rate.

### Trick Room
- Has the lowest priority (-7).

### Powder Moves
Fail against:
- Grass-types
- Overcoat
- Safety Goggles

---

## Ability Interactions

### Mold Breaker
Can ignore many defensive abilities (e.g. Levitate, Sturdy).

### Soundproof
Blocks sound-based moves.

### Bulletproof
Blocks bomb and ball moves.

### Overcoat
Blocks Powder moves.

### Dancer
Automatically copies another Pokémon's dance move.

---

## Item Interactions

### Safety Goggles
- Immune to Powder moves.
- Immune to Sandstorm damage.

### Covert Cloak
- Prevents additional move effects.

### Mental Herb
- Removes Taunt, Encore, Disable, Torment, Heal Block, and Attract once.

### Clear Amulet
- Prevents stat drops caused by opponents.

---

## Double Battle

### Spread Moves
- Deal reduced damage when hitting multiple targets.

### Redirection
- Rage Powder does not affect Grass-types, Overcoat, or Safety Goggles.
- Some moves (e.g. Snipe Shot) ignore redirection.
